MOSUN — Bio

SHORT BIO

MOSUN (Morrison Ma) is a Chinese-American artist and producer — born in Guangzhou, raised in the US, based between Taipei and Los Angeles. He writes, produces and arranges his own records around a modular synth rig, singing in Mandarin and English: dark, cinematic R&B for the hours between midnight and sunrise. A Recording Academy voting member, he has arranged for BLACKPINK’s Coachella set and for some of the biggest names in Mandopop, including Jolin Tsai, and is a 2026 Golden Melody Awards nominee for Best Arranger.
